# Client ki exact location kaise add karni hai (App)

Naya client banate waqt **Place** aur **Exact location** dono zaruri hain. Location Google Maps se aati hai, aur check-in selfie isi se milaya jaata hai. Isliye **dukaan ya office ke exact darwaze** par pin hona chahiye.

---

## Rep ke liye: 5 kadam

### 1. Pehle naam aur place likho
Client form mein **Client name** (jaise `Agarwal Brothers`) aur **Place** (jaise `Jaipur`) bharo.

### 2. "Search on Google Maps" dabao
Form ke **Exact location** hisse mein ye button hai. Phone ki **Google Maps app** khulti hai, aur search mein client ka naam aur place pehle se likha hota hai.

### 3. Sahi dukaan chuno
List mein se **sahi** dukaan ya office par tap karo, taaki uska poora page khule. Kai baar ek hi naam ki do-teen dukaanen hoti hain, to address dekh kar chuno.

### 4. Share, phir Copy link
Us dukaan ke page par **Share** dabao, phir **Copy link** (ya **Copy**).

### 5. App mein wapas aao, "Paste the copied link" dabao
App mein wapas aakar **Paste the copied link** dabao. App link ko padhta hai aur location apne aap bhar deta hai. Upar hari patti mein dikhta hai:

> **Found "Agarwal Brothers": 26.91240, 75.78730.** Check that this is the right place, then save.

Naam sahi dikhe to **Save client** dabao. Ho gaya.

---

## Agar kuch na chale

| Dikkat | Kya karo |
|---|---|
| "Paste the copied link" dabane par kuch nahi hota | Neeche wale box mein ungli **dabaye rakho**, **Paste** chuno. Link apne aap padha jaata hai. |
| "Could not reach the office" | Internet check karo. Link padhne ke liye internet zaruri hai. |
| "Could not find the place in this link" | Google Maps mein **dukaan ka page** kholo (sirf search list nahi), phir Share → Copy link. |
| Internet nahi hai | Google Maps mein dukaan par **dabaye rakho** (long-press). Upar jo do number dikhte hain (jaise `26.9124, 75.7873`) wo copy karke box mein paste karo. Internet ki zaroorat nahi. |
| Galat dukaan mili | Dobara "Search on Google Maps" dabao aur sahi dukaan chuno. Naya link paste karte hi purani location badal jaati hai. |

---

## Zaroori baatein
- Location ek baar office (manager) ne website par set kar di, to wo **lock** ho jaati hai. Rep use nahi badal sakta. Form mein likha aata hai "Set by the office".
- Rep ko apni **current location** se client nahi bana sakte (jaan-boojh kar), taaki office mein baithkar sab clients set na ho paayein.
- Jab rep client ke paas **check-in selfie** leta hai, tab selfie ki location is exact pin se milayi jaati hai. 300 metre se zyada door ho to "Away from client" ka flag lagta hai.

# Manager ke liye (Website)

Sales team → **Clients** → **Add client** (ya Contacts page par **Add a client with location**).

1. Client ka **naam** aur **Place** bharo.
2. Google Maps mein dukaan kholo, **Share → Copy link**.
3. **Exact location** ke box mein link **paste** karo (ya numbers jaise `26.9124, 75.7873`). Ek second mein **Latitude** aur **Longitude** apne aap bhar jaate hain, aur hari notification mein dukaan ka naam dikhta hai.
4. Box ke dahine taraf **location pin** ka button Google Maps naye tab mein kholta hai.
5. **Create** dabao.

Website par jo location manager daalta hai, wo "office ki" maani jaati hai. Rep ka phone use badal nahi sakta.
